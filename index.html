Hi, I'm Shohruh.

Curiosity has taken me further than certainty ever could.

Student, builder, founder— curious.

So I keep learning, building, and asking bigger questions.

I occasionally write my thoughts in essays and keep notes from the books that have changed the way I think. I believe curiosity compounds when you learn, reflect, and share what you've learned.

Reading and writing won't be enough at times. I study the subject inside out if I need to. I do deep dives.

When an idea stays in my head for too long, I try to turn it into something real. Sometimes it's a website, sometimes a tool, sometimes just an experiment. You can find a few of those in my projects.

Away from my computer, I usually trade code for movement. I run, work out, and occasionally escape into nature whenever I need to think more clearly. Some of those moments end up in my journal.

I don't think a single title can describe a person. Student, builder, founder—they're all works in progress. Curiosity is probably the only label I'm happy to keep.

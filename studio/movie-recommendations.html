Movie Recommendations

Films that stuck with me, worth your time too.

Stranger Things

Created by the Duffer Brothers

IMDb: 8.6/10

A love letter to 80s sci-fi and horror — a small Indiana town, a missing kid, and a growing tangle of government secrets and things that shouldn't exist.

Interstellar

Directed by Christopher Nolan

IMDb: 8.7/10

A dying Earth, a father-daughter bond stretched across galaxies, and one of the most emotionally devastating uses of time dilation in film.

Game of Thrones

Created by David Benioff & D. B. Weiss

IMDb: 9.2/10

Power, politics, and war across the Seven Kingdoms — ruthless in how it treats its characters, and unforgettable because of it.

Inception

Directed by Christopher Nolan

IMDb: 8.8/10

A heist movie set inside dreams within dreams. Endlessly rewatchable, and the kind of film that rewards paying close attention to every detail.

The Odyssey

Directed by Christopher Nolan

IMDb Top 250 (2026)

Nolan's adaptation of Homer's epic, following Odysseus's long, perilous journey home after the Trojan War. Shot entirely on IMAX 70mm — one of his most acclaimed films yet.

Friends

Created by David Crane & Marta Kauffman

IMDb: 8.9/10

Six friends navigating life, love, and coffee shops in Manhattan. Pure comfort TV — a show I can rewatch endlessly without ever getting tired of it.

The Dark Knight

Directed by Christopher Nolan

IMDb: 9.0/10

Less a superhero movie than a crime saga about order versus chaos. Heath Ledger's Joker is one of the greatest villain performances ever put on screen.

Fight Club

Directed by David Fincher

IMDb: 8.8/10

A sharp, unsettling look at masculinity, consumerism, and identity. The kind of movie that hits differently on a second watch once you know where it's going.

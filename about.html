About

I don't know exactly what I'll build in ten years. I just know I want to spend those ten years becoming someone capable of building it.

01Curiosity

I still haven't decided what one subject I want to dedicate my life to. Maybe that's why I keep learning everything that genuinely interests me—economics, psychology, politics, technology, history, and whatever else makes me stop and ask, "Why does this work the way it does?" I write about some of those thoughts in essays, mostly so I can understand them a little better myself.

02Doing it myself

I have a habit of wanting to figure things out on my own. Whether it's building a website, learning a new skill, or solving a problem, my first instinct is usually to try it myself before asking for help. Sometimes that's a strength. Sometimes it's probably my biggest weakness.

03People

I've also realized I quietly care a lot about the people around me. I enjoy seeing others succeed, even if I rarely say it out loud. Maybe that's why I'm drawn to building things that make someone's life a little easier.

04For now

For now, I'm simply trying to stay curious. I trust that if I keep learning, writing, and building, the path will eventually reveal itself.

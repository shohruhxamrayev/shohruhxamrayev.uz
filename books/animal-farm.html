Animal Farm

George Orwell

Read: January 6, 2026 • Rating: 6/10

The first fictional book I've read and completed.

The book was originally written to mimic the establishment of the Soviet Union after the Bolshevik Revolution that ended the rule of Tsar Nicholas II.

In the beginning, the working class is happy that now they will take control of the country, but then some of the comrades become avaricious and eventually turn the state into something it used to be before.

Likewise, animals throw away the monarchy and create a heavenly republic, but pigs get to power and let their greed take over the primary idea of their intent. Every character represents individuals in the real-world country.

Orwell mocks human hunger for power and money in a metaphorical way, depicting individuals as animals and the state as a farm.

It also includes elements of the aggressive form of propaganda, like the one that was used during the Soviet era. A very interesting way of delivery. Nearly a perfect metaphorical depiction of propaganda.

Overall, it's a great book, but nothing is that surprising. I didn't learn much, but it was extremely interesting.

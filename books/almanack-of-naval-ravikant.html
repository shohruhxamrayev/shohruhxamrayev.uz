The Almanack of Naval Ravikant

Naval Ravikant

Read: June 25, 2025 • Rating: 8/10

The Almanack of Naval Ravikant is a curated collection of wisdom from entrepreneur and investor Naval Ravikant, focused on building wealth, cultivating happiness, and living a meaningful life. Compiled by Eric Jorgenson, the book distills Naval's interviews, tweets, and essays into clear, actionable principles on decision-making, lifelong learning, leverage, and personal well-being. It serves as a practical guide for anyone seeking financial freedom and inner peace.
